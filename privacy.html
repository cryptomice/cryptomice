🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cryptomice handles user information when you use our platform, including both the Free Section and the Paid Referral Section.

We collect only the information necessary to operate the platform:

  • Email address (for account creation and authentication)

  • Device and usage information (for security, fraud prevention, and performance purposes)

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To track referral performance and rewards
  • To process withdrawals and payment requests
  • To monitor free-section reward activities
  • To detect abuse, duplicate accounts, bots, or suspicious activity
  • To improve platform security and system stability

Cryptomice currently operates multiple participation systems:

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies and operational standards.

Certain platform activities may involve engagement tracking systems to verify valid task completion.

  • Referral verification
  • Anti-fraud monitoring

Invalid, automated, manipulated, or fraudulent activity may result in rejected rewards, suspended withdrawals, or account restrictions.

We implement reasonable technical and administrative security measures to protect user data and maintain platform integrity.

Cryptomice does not sell personal user data. Information may only be shared when necessary to:
