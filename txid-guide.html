🔎 What is TXID?

Cryptomice Payment Verification Guide

💡 TXID Explained

A TXID (Transaction ID) is a unique tracking number for any crypto payment. It works like a receipt number that proves your transaction exists on the blockchain.

Every time you send Litecoin (LTC), the system creates a TXID automatically.

📌 Why TXID is Important

✔ It proves you sent payment
✔ It allows verification on blockchain
✔ It links sender → receiver
✔ It confirms transaction time and amount

📍 How to Get Your TXID

Depending on your wallet:

Binance: Wallet → Transaction History → Click withdrawal → “View on Blockchain” → Copy TXID
Trust Wallet: Litecoin → Select transaction → More Details → Copy TXID
Coinbase: Activity → Open transaction → View on Explorer → Copy TXID
Any wallet: Look for “Transaction ID” or “Hash”

⚠️ Important Note

Make sure you copy the correct TXID. If it is wrong, your payment cannot be verified.

📥 How Cryptomice Uses It

We use your TXID to verify your Litecoin payment before activating your account. This ensures only real payments are approved.
