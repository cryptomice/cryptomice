How Cryptomice Works

Cryptomice is a digital engagement platform where users participate in structured referral and activity-based features. The system is designed to track participation and reward user engagement through platform-defined rules.

📊 Platform Overview

When a user joins through a referral link, they are guided through an activation process. Once completed, the system records the referral relationship and enables access to platform features.

🧩 System Structure

FREE ACCESS TIER

The free access tier includes high-frequency engagement features such as ads, faucets, and offer-based tasks. These are designed to allow broader participation across users.

PREMIUM ACCESS TIER

The premium access tier provides enhanced engagement opportunities with fewer but higher-value activities. This tier is intended for users who participate more actively within the platform ecosystem.

Both tiers operate under the same system rules and are designed to maintain balanced platform activity.

🚀 Getting Started

1. Create an account
2. Access your referral link
3. Share and invite participants
4. Track engagement through your dashboard

🌍 Why Users Join

Users participate in Cryptomice to explore structured digital engagement systems, referral tracking, and reward-based interaction features within the platform.

⚠ Important Information

Platform rewards are based on internal rules, engagement activity, and verification systems. Participation levels may vary depending on user activity.
